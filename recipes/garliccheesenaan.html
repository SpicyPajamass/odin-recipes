Ingredients

Dough

  • 2 cups AP flour
  • 1/2 tbsp instant yeast
  • 1/2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/2 cup full fat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp melted butter

Cheese

  • 1 cup shredded mozarella

Garlic Butter

  • 1 stick melted butter
  • 5 cloves minced garlic
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
