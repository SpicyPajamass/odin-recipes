Red Pepper Hummus

Home

Description

I've always loved a good batch of Red Pepper Hummus. I usually modify my Great Grandpa, David Haddad's, recipe, but I used a online recipe for the sake of the project. Garlicy, creamy, and great with naan or pita bread!

Ingredients

  • 1 16 ounce can chickpeas
  • 1/2 cup roasted red pepper
  • 1/4 cup aquafaba
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 1/8 teaspoon cumin
  • Salt to taste
